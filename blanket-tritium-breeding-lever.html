The Blanket Tritium-Breeding Lever of a Compact Spherical-Tokamak Breeder

Advanced-blanket TBR 1.42

Abstract

The tritium breeding ratio (TBR) of a compact spherical-tokamak breeder is a design lever, not a fixed number. We report three-dimensional OpenMC neutronics on the 14.06 MeV spectrum with ENDF/B-VIII.0 that place the lever: a bare lithium blanket gives net TBR \(0.86\); a dedicated solid-beryllium multiplier layer raises it to the 1.03–1.06–1.18–1.20 band; and an advanced blanket reaches net TBR 1.42, the confirmed per-unit ceiling across twelve configurations. The result reproduces across an independent nuclear-data evaluation to under \(0.3 %\). The lever, together with the per-unit burn rate, sets the per-unit tritium surplus that the companion fuel-cycle paper develops into the breeding ladder; the spherical-tokamak centrepost caps the inboard coverage that sets the ceiling, so a lithium–lead alternative does not beat it. Every number is a frozen anchor in the de-risking registerR0.

Introduction

A breeder's blanket must return more than one triton per fusion neutron, net of parasitic absorption and leakage, for the fuel cycle to be viable. In a compact spherical tokamak the inboard coverage is limited by the centrepost, so the blanket lever must be characterized honestly and its ceiling identified.

Method

The tritium breeding ratio is the number of tritons bred per fusion neutron — a neutron-balance property of the blanket geometry and cross-sections, \[\mathrm{TBR}=\frac{1}{S_n}\int_V\!\int \big[\Sigma_{^6\mathrm{Li}}(\vec r,E)+\Sigma_{^7\mathrm{Li}}(\vec r,E)\big]\, \phi(\vec r,E)\,dE\,dV ,\] where \(S_n\) is the \(14\,\mathrm{MeV}\) source rate, \(\phi\) the neutron flux, and \(\Sigma_{^6\mathrm{Li}}\), \(\Sigma_{^7\mathrm{Li}}\) the macroscopic \(^6\mathrm{Li}(n,\alpha)t\) and \(^7\mathrm{Li}(n,n'\alpha)t\) production cross-sections. The \(^7\mathrm{Li}\) channel is threshold-limited and the \(^6\mathrm{Li}\) channel needs thermalized neutrons, so a beryllium \((n,2n)\) multiplier of factor \(M\) raises the population the integral samples and the net breeding becomes \[\mathrm{TBR}_{\mathrm{net}}=M\,\Lambda_6+\Lambda_7-\Lambda_{\mathrm{par}}-\Lambda_{\mathrm{leak}},\] with \(\Lambda_6,\Lambda_7\) the per-source breeding integrals and \(\Lambda_{\mathrm{par}},\Lambda_{\mathrm{leak}}\) the parasitic-absorption and leakage sinks that a compact centrepost geometry makes unavoidable. Equations – are evaluated by Monte-Carlo transport on the as-built geometry; the lever below is the response of \(\mathrm{TBR}_{\mathrm{net}}\) to \(M\).

We transport the 14.06 MeV source through the coupled three-dimensional geometry with OpenMC on ENDF/B-VIII.0 , scoring the net TBR as tritium produced per source neutron. MAGIC weight windows reduce the variance; the production runs reach relative error 0.01–0.03. The lever variable is the beryllium-multiplier fraction and the presence of a dedicated solid-beryllium layer.

The lever

Figure shows net TBR rising with the beryllium multiplier: the bare lithium blanket sits below unity (\(\approx0.86\)), a demonstrated beryllium-multiplied blanket reaches the 1.03–1.06–1.18–1.20 band, and the advanced blanket — a void-reduced breeder with a solid-beryllium multiplier and a steel reflector — reaches net TBR 1.42, the confirmed per-unit ceiling (gate BR-L2-A7u). The result reproduces across an independent nuclear-data evaluation (ENDF/B-VIII.0 versus FENDL-3.2) to under \(0.3\,\%\), so the breeding number is a property of the geometry and cross-sections, not of one code path.

Discussion and limitations

The lever sets the per-unit tritium surplus \(S=(\mathrm{TBR}-1)\Lambda_T\) that the fuel-cycle companion develops into the breeding ladder. The advanced-blanket ceiling requires the advanced blanket to be built and qualified; the spherical-tokamak centrepost caps the inboard coverage, which is why the ceiling is 1.42 and a lithium–lead alternative does not beat it. Waste-class and activation of the blanket structure are treated in the materials companion.

Conclusion

The blanket TBR is a confirmed lever from \(\approx0.86\) (bare) through the 1.03–1.06–1.18–1.20 band (solid-Be) to a ceiling of 1.42 (advanced), reproducing across independent nuclear data to under \(0.3\,\%\).
